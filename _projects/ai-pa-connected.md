**Artificial Intelligence for Public Administrations Connected** is a European Digital Innovation Hub run by SDA Bocconi, Maggioli Group, University of Bologna, University of Milano-Bicocca, IFEL, and Tempo S.r.l. It helps public administrations, small and medium enterprises, and startups try AI solutions before investing in them.

**My role:** development of training services and test-before-invest services focused on retrieval-augmented chatbots that run locally, with small open models and limited data.
