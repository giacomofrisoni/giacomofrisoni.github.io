**Duration:** Mar 2023 – May 2026 · **Funder:** Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy · **Competitive call:** NextGenerationEU (winner of the Seal of Excellence award) · **Total budget:** ~€5.8 million

Artificial Intelligence for Public Administrations Connected.
A consortium with SDA Bocconi, Maggioli Group, University of Bologna, University of Milano-Bicocca, IFEL, and Tempo S.r.l. supporting public administrations, small and medium enterprises, and startups in piloting AI-based solutions.

**My role:** Assistance in the development of training services and test-before-invest services focused on retrieval-augmented chatbots in low-resource regimes.
